Nightwave now makes karaoke, album artwork, playlists, and keyboard playback easier to use.

### What's new

- Open **Karaoke** beside **Play all** to go straight to the Midnight visualizer with karaoke enabled. Your saved karaoke mode and word-highlighting preferences are kept.
- Follow embedded timed lyrics with scrolling lines or a current-line display, and turn word highlighting on or off independently.
- When a song has no timed lyrics, Nightwave suggests [Tracksmith](https://github.com/tefaz/Tracksmith-mp3-enricher) or another tool that embeds standard SYLT or LRC lyrics.
- Click the footer cover or any song's thumbnail to view the original full-resolution artwork in a centered, frameless overlay. Press Escape or click to close it.
- The footer cover fills its height and sits flush against the left edge, without a glow or a taller footer.
- Right-click a song and choose **Edit title**, **Edit artist**, or **Edit album** to open the inline editor.
- **Add to playlist** now shows a vertical list of playlist-name buttons. Click one to add the song immediately.
- Press **Space** to toggle Play/Pause, **Enter** to play the selected song from the beginning, and **Up/Down** to move through the song list. Text fields and dialogs keep their normal keyboard behavior.
- Automatic song changes center the playing song. Choosing a song or pressing Next/Previous keeps your browsing position.

### Downloads

- **AppImage:** download it, make it executable, and open it.
- **Debian/Ubuntu:** install the `.deb` package.
