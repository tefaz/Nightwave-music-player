Nightwave now has three music-reactive visualizers, simpler header menus, and easier song dragging.

### What's new

- Open **View → Visualizer** to enjoy Space tunnel, Aurora, or Kaleidoscope. Use the arrow on the right to switch effects. The header and playback controls stay visible, and Nightwave remembers your selection.
- Show or hide the live audio spectrum below your playlists with **View → Show equalizer**.
- Find folder loading, folder refresh, folder management, file imports, and library clearing together in the **Files** menu.
- Drag from anywhere on a song row to add it to a playlist or open its source file in another desktop app. Select several songs to drag them together.
- Search for album artwork from a song's menu and save a chosen cover into its MP3 file.
- Save inline title, artist, and album edits with Enter or by clicking outside the editor. Press Escape to cancel.
- A clearer speaker icon marks the volume control, and the README screenshot shows the updated interface.

### Downloads

- **AppImage:** download it, make it executable, and open it.
- **Debian/Ubuntu:** install the `.deb` package.
