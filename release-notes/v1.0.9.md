Nightwave makes karaoke easier to find and gives it a quiet stage of its own.

### What's new

- A subtle marker between **Time** and the song menu shows which songs have embedded timed lyrics. It recognizes standard SYLT and LRC timing, and checks older library entries in the background.
- **Karaoke** now sits in the top bar, so you can open it without scrolling back to the top of your music library.
- **Now playing** is renamed to **Go to playing**, keeping the same shortcut to the current song.
- Opening Karaoke selects the new **Karaoke lounge** visualizer: soft amber spotlights, dark curtains, and a subtle microphone silhouette. Like Midnight, it stays still so the lyrics remain easy to follow.
- Karaoke lounge also appears in the visualizer cycle alongside Space tunnel, Midnight, Aurora, and Kaleidoscope. Your karaoke mode and word-highlighting preferences are kept.

### Downloads

- **AppImage:** download it, make it executable, and open it.
- **Debian/Ubuntu:** install the `.deb` package.
