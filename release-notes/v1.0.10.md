Nightwave can now cast your local music and karaoke lyrics to a Chromecast TV using Google's default receiver.

### What's new

- A **Cast** button beside the volume slider finds TVs and speakers on your local network. Play/pause, seeking, volume, shuffle, and the playback queue continue to work from Nightwave.
- **Music only** is the default casting mode and streams the original audio file directly from your computer.
- Three TV karaoke modes offer a current line, scrolling lines with fades, or scrolling with word highlights. Their colors, typography, and movement follow Nightwave's karaoke view. Word highlights use embedded word timings when available, with estimates for songs that only have line timings.
- TV karaoke uses temporary videos, so it works with the default receiver without installing a custom TV app. FFmpeg is required on the computer; Nightwave shows preparation progress and keeps a small session cache. Songs without timed lyrics cast as audio.
- **Firewall help** explains how to let the selected device reach Nightwave. On Linux with UFW and PolicyKit, **Allow this device** offers an authenticated fix scoped to that device and the streaming port, with a copyable command and retry option.
- Footer **Next** and **Previous** center the new song when shuffle is on. With shuffle off, they scroll down or up by one song row.

### Downloads

- **AppImage:** download it, make it executable, and open it.
- **Debian/Ubuntu:** install the `.deb` package.

Your computer must stay running while casting. Music-only casting does not require FFmpeg.
